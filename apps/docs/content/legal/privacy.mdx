## Introduction

LibravelUI is the product and documentation experience for developer-facing UI components and related resources. It is operated by Libravelabs, the organization responsible for maintaining and publishing the project. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the site, read documentation, interact with the project, or use any services that are made available through the platform.

This policy is intended to be clear, practical, and aligned with the expectations of modern developer products, open-source communities, and software documentation sites.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with LibravelUI, we may collect:

- Personal information you provide directly, such as your name or contact details when you contact the project team or submit a support request.
- Account or profile information if account-based features or services are enabled by Libravelabs.
- Email addresses or other contact details associated with communications, support, or account-related activity.
- Billing or payment information if premium resources or paid offerings are made available. In such cases, the minimum information necessary to complete the transaction may be collected by the payment processor used by the operator.
- Automatically collected information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and usage analytics that helps the team understand product performance and improve the user experience.

## How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve LibravelUI and its documentation experience.
- Support users and respond to inquiries.
- Process payments for premium resources if those offerings are available.
- Prevent misuse, abuse, fraud, or other harmful activity.
- Send important updates about the project, service changes, or security matters.

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The site may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how visitors use the site. These technologies may also help the project team improve performance and content relevance.

If analytics tools are enabled, they may collect aggregated information about site interactions. You may be able to control or clear cookies through your browser settings, subject to the capabilities of your device and browser.

## Third-party Services

LibravelUI may rely on third-party services for hosting, authentication, analytics, or payment processing where those services are configured by Libravelabs. The specific providers may vary by deployment and are not assumed here. If a specific provider is used, it will be disclosed in the relevant public documentation or deployment notices.

This policy does not assume or invent providers that are not explicitly disclosed by the operator.

## Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer needed, it will be deleted, anonymized, or archived in a manner consistent with applicable law and operational requirements.

## Data Security

Libravelabs will use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and users should exercise care when sharing sensitive information online.

## User Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities or request a copy of your information in a portable format where required by law.

To exercise these rights, please use the public contact channel published by Libravelabs for the project.

## Children’s Privacy

LibravelUI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian consent where required by law.

## International Users

If you access LibravelUI from outside the jurisdiction where the operator is based, your information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in another country. By using the service, you consent to such transfers where permitted by applicable law and the controls adopted by the operator.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Libravelabs may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, the revised version will be posted on the site and should be reviewed by visitors and users.

## Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact the Libravelabs team through the official project channels published on the LibravelUI website.
